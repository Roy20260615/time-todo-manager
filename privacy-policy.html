English

My Time List is a personal productivity app for managing tasks, time-flow logs, idea notes, local backups, PDF reports, and encrypted backup files.

1. Data Stored in the App

The app may store tasks, logs, idea notes, categories, priorities, due dates, backup history, backup notes, and app settings on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's local features.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect personal data from the app, do not track you, and do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. Backups and Exported Files

The app allows you to export JSON backups, encrypted JSON backups, and PDF reports. Exported files are created on your device and are managed by you. If you share or store these files outside the app, you are responsible for where they are saved and who can access them.

4. Encrypted Backups

If you create an encrypted backup, the backup is protected with the password you set. We do not know or store your backup password. If the password is lost, the encrypted backup cannot be recovered by the developer.

5. In-App Purchases

The app may offer a one-time purchase for advanced backup features. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

6. Internet Access

The app's core features are designed to work locally. The app includes a link to this privacy policy, which may open in your system browser. StoreKit may contact Apple's services to load product information, complete purchases, and restore purchases.

7. Customer Support

If you contact us by email, we will use the information you provide only to respond to your question or support request.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the support email listed on the App Store product page.
